Motte & Bailey Restaurant is set at Mount Temple House in Moate, County Westmeath.

The dining room pairs rich burgundy tones, traditional wooden furniture and warm lighting to create an intimate setting for a meal and a conversation. Whether you are planning a relaxed evening or a special occasion, contact the restaurant directly to discuss your visit.

We keep this page focused on the setting and experience rather than making unverified claims about the restaurant's history, menu or awards.
