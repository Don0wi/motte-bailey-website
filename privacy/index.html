INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

A clear explanation of how enquiry information may be handled.

Draft policy — the restaurant should review and complete this notice before accepting real customer information.

Information you provide

If you send a reservation, event or contact enquiry, the site may process your name, contact details, requested date, party size and message so the restaurant can respond.

Purpose and lawful basis

Information should be used only to respond to your request and, where applicable, manage a booking. The restaurant must determine and document the appropriate lawful basis under applicable data-protection law.

Retention and access

Enquiry data should be accessible only to authorised staff and retained only as long as necessary for the stated purpose or applicable legal obligations. A real deployment must specify retention periods and deletion procedures.

Service providers

Hosting, database, email and security providers may process data on the restaurant's behalf. The operator should document these providers and any relevant international transfers before launch.

Your rights and contact

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing. Contact the restaurant using the published telephone number to ask how to exercise applicable rights. A verified privacy contact should be added before launch.

Cookies and analytics

Any non-essential analytics or tracking should be configured with appropriate notice and consent where required. Do not enable tracking until the restaurant has reviewed the settings.

Updates

This draft should be updated to reflect the actual production system and reviewed by a qualified professional before publication.
